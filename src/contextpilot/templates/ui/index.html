What Ctxly (ContextPilot) does

AI coding assistants like Google Antigravity and Gemini CLI are powerful but forgetful. If you stop them or they run out of memory, they start over. They also often read your whole project, which is slow and costs money. Ctxly sits next to them and fixes that:

RemembersSaves what you were doing, so the AI picks up where it left off.
FocusesPoints the AI at the 2-4 files that matter, so it reads less and costs less.
ProtectsHides passwords and keys before anything is saved or sent.

"Ctxly" is the short name. You'll also see "ContextPilot" and the command ctxly. They're all the same thing.

Get started in 5 minutes

  1. Start the appDouble-click Start ContextPilot.cmd (Windows) or start-contextpilot.command (Mac). The first time, it installs what it needs (you need Python 3.10 or newer). Your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:8765. Keep the black window open while you use the app.
  2. Open your projectClick Browse for folder… and choose the folder that holds your code, or paste its path.
  3. Connect your AI toolClick Connect both (or just the one you use). Then close and reopen your AI tool so it notices. You can always do this later in Setup.
  4. Ask for somethingOn Ask AI, describe what you want like you'd tell a colleague, then click Prepare my request. Click Copy instructions, paste them into your AI tool (Ctrl+V, or ⌘V on Mac) and press Enter.
  5. Check the resultWhen the AI says it's done, come back and use What did the AI change?, Check if it works, or Undo AI changes if you don't like it.

A normal day with Ctxly

1 · DescribeType or say (🎤) what you need. Paste a screenshot of an error if you have one.
2 · SendCopy the prepared instructions into your AI, or click Send to Gemini CLI / Open in Antigravity.
3 · CheckSee what changed, run your tests, undo if needed. If tests fail, send the "fix-it" request back.
4 · SaveStopping for the day? Write two lines in Save progress. Tomorrow it's waiting for you.

Tips for good requests: mention the error message, the screen or feature name, and what "fixed" looks like. "Users get logged out after 5 minutes on the dashboard, they should stay signed in for a day" works much better than "fix login".

If the AI gets interrupted

If you started Gemini CLI from this app, pressing Ctrl+C or closing it saves your progress automatically. If your AI tool is connected (step 3 above), it also saves before its memory fills up.

Next time you open the app, a Welcome back card shows what you were doing, what you'd figured out and the next step. Click Copy "where I left off" note and paste it into the AI. It continues without you explaining everything again.

Going back in time

Every time you click Prepare my request, Ctxly quietly saves a copy of your files: an undo point. Undo AI changes puts your files back to that moment.

The Timeline lists every saved moment. Click See changes to look, or Restore to here to go back. Before going back, Ctxly always saves your current files as a "Safety copy" entry, so you can change your mind.

Going back never touches password files (like .env), installed packages, or your team notes.

Where your data lives

Everything stays on your computer. Your saved work (tasks, notes, next steps, the list of changed files, test results, savings) is kept in a small local database file inside your project: .ctxly/sessions.db (SQLite).

.ctxly/sessions.dbYour memory: sessions, saved moments, decisions, to-dos, known problems, test results, savings.
.ctxly/config.jsonYour settings for this project.
Undo copiesStored compactly by git, hidden from your normal history.
.ctxly-team/Only what you choose to share with your team.

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on Gemini smart mode. Then only the text needed for that feature (your request, the small parts of code it picked, or a screenshot you added) goes to Google's Gemini, with passwords and keys hidden first.

You choose how that works in Setup → What may leave your computer: Automatically, Ask me first (you see exactly what would be sent and click Send), or Never.

Password files like .env, private keys and credential files are never read at all.

How the money numbers work

AI tools charge by how much text they read. Ctxly compares "the AI reads your whole project" with "the AI reads only the files Ctxly picked" and turns the difference into money at Google's list prices. These are estimates. When smart mode is on, the Savings page also shows what Google actually billed for Ctxly's own Gemini calls.

You can switch between dollars and rupees, and set a spending limit: Gemini CLI then pauses and saves your progress when a session gets too expensive.

Common questions

Do I need to know git or programming?

No. If your folder doesn't use git yet, Setup → Health check offers a "Turn on" button that sets it up for you. Your files aren't changed.

Is my code uploaded anywhere?

Not unless you turn on Gemini smart mode, and even then only the small parts needed, with secrets hidden. Everything else stays in your project's .ctxly folder.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything except Gemini smart mode works offline.

Why is "Send to Gemini CLI" greyed out?

Gemini CLI isn't installed on this computer. Use Copy instructions and paste into any AI chat instead, or see Setup → Health check for how to install it.

The page says "ContextPilot was restarted".

Close the tab and open http://127.0.0.1:8765 again.

The page won't open.

Make sure the black ContextPilot window is still open. If you closed it, double-click Start ContextPilot.cmd again. Use the address http://127.0.0.1:8765, not a file on your disk.

I undid something by mistake.

Open Timeline, find the "Safety copy" entry just before it and click Restore to here.

"Check if it works" says no tests were found.

Your project doesn't have automated tests yet, or uses an unusual command. Ask the AI "write tests for …", or type your test command in Setup → Tests.
